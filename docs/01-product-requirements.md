# SLGS Digital Platform — Product Requirements

## 1. Vision

Create a maintainable digital platform for Sierra Leone Grammar School that modernizes the public web presence while introducing a secure administrative S.I.M.S. platform and a dedicated ICT/STEM asset-management capability.

## 2. Problems

The existing public website is not sufficiently functional, structured, or maintained. Content updates should not depend on developers.

The school also needs a private operational platform for administration and ICT/STEM equipment lifecycle management.

## 3. Product principles

- Separate public communication from private administration.
- Make routine content management possible without developer intervention.
- Give student clubs controlled publishing capabilities.
- Moderate student-generated public content.
- Make sensitive administrative data private by default.
- Design for future expansion without coupling all domains together.
- Prefer boring, understandable technology over unnecessary complexity.
