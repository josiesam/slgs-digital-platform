# S.I.M.S. & Asset Management User Guide

Welcome to the **Sierra Leone Grammar School Information Management System (S.I.M.S.)** user guide. This operational handbook provides step-by-step instructions for school administrators, academic staff, form teachers, and ICT/STEM lab managers.

---

## 1. User Roles & Modules

Access to S.I.M.S. is strictly controlled based on your assigned school role:

| School Role | Modules Accessible | Primary Tasks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **School Administrator** | All Modules (Students, Staff, Classes, Attendance, Assets, Audit) | • Enroll new students & assign form classes<br/>• Create staff accounts & assign roles<br/>• Manage academic sessions & terms<br/>• View global attendance & security logs |
| **Form Teacher / Academic Staff** | My Classes, Attendance, Student Directory, Reports | • Mark daily morning & afternoon attendance<br/>• View student emergency contact details<br/>• Submit attendance corrections<br/>• Generate class rosters & attendance summaries |
| **ICT / STEM Asset Manager** | Asset Inventory, Lab Allocations, Maintenance, Repairs | • Track computer labs, robotics, & STEM equipment<br/>• Update equipment lifecycle states (In Use, Repair, Retired)<br/>• Log maintenance & repair work orders<br/>• Manage equipment allocation to rooms & staff |

---

## 2. Getting Started & Dashboard Navigation

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the S.I.M.S. portal (`http://sims.slgs.edu.sl` or `http://localhost:3000` during testing).
2. Log in using your authorized school administrative credentials.
3. The **S.I.M.S. Dashboard** presents an overview tailored to your role:
   - **Form Teachers**: Displays today's attendance status for your assigned class (*"Form 4A — Attendance Pending"*).
   - **ICT Staff**: Displays active equipment count, items under repair, and upcoming scheduled maintenance.
   - **School Admins**: Displays total active student enrollment, staff counts, and recent system audit alerts.

## 3. Student & Staff Management Guide

### Adding & Enrolling a Student
1. Navigate to **Students** → **Enroll New Student**.
2. Enter the student's personal details:
   - **Registration / Admission Number**: Official school ID (e.g., `SLGS-2026-0142`).
   - **Personal Info**: First name, last name, date of birth, gender.
   - **Class Assignment**: Select Form Level (e.g., *Form 3*) and Arm/Section (e.g., *Arm B*).
   - **Guardian Contacts**: Primary parent/guardian name, phone number, email, and home address.
3. Click **Save & Enroll Student**.

### Managing Class Rosters & Form Groups
1. Go to **Classes & Arms**. Select a class (e.g., *Form 5 Science*).
2. View the full student roster, assigned Form Master / Teacher, and subject list.
3. Use **Reassign Class** at the start of a new academic year to promote students to the next form level.

### Staff Directory & Account Linkage
1. Go to **Staff Directory**.
2. Administrators can add new staff records, assign designations (*Head of STEM*, *Form Master*, *Bursar*), and link the record to an authorized S.I.M.S. user login account.

## 4. Daily Attendance Operations

*For Form Teachers and Academic Staff.*

### Marking Daily Class Attendance
1. Click **Daily Attendance** on your dashboard.
2. Select your assigned Form Class and the active Session/Term.
3. The class roster is loaded. For each student, select their status:
   - 🟢 **Present**: Student is in class.
   - 🔴 **Absent**: Unexcused absence.
   - 🟡 **Late**: Arrived after morning assembly.
   - 🔵 **Excused**: Absence pre-approved (e.g., medical leave).
4. Click **Submit Attendance**. The record is locked with a timestamp and your digital signature.

### Correcting an Attendance Record
If a student was mistakenly marked absent but arrived with a medical note:
1. Go to **Attendance History** and locate the date.
2. Click **Request Correction**.
3. Select the new status (e.g., change `Absent` → `Excused`).
4. **Mandatory Note**: Type the reason (e.g., *"Medical note submitted by parent"*).
5. Click **Submit Correction**. The system updates the status while preserving the original record in the permanent audit trail for compliance.

## 5. ICT & STEM Asset Management Guide

*For ICT Coordinators, Computer Lab Technicians, and STEM Staff.*

### Registering New Equipment
1. Navigate to **Asset Inventory** → **+ Register New Asset**.
2. Enter asset details:
   - **Asset Tag ID**: Scan or enter the unique physical barcode/tag (e.g., `STEM-LAB-042`).
   - **Serial Number**: Manufacturer serial number.
   - **Category**: Select *Computers*, *Laptops*, *Tablets*, *Networking*, *Robotics*, *Arduino*, *Lab Equipment*, or *Consumables*.
   - **Make & Model**: (e.g., *HP ProDesk 400 G6* or *Arduino Uno Rev3 Kit*).
   - **Acquisition Info**: Purchase date, cost, supplier name, warranty expiration date.
   - **Initial Location**: Select room (e.g., *Computer Lab 1*, *STEM Lab*, *Principal's Office*).
3. Click **Save Asset Record**.

### Managing Asset Lifecycle States
Every piece of equipment moves through 10 recognized lifecycle states:

```
[ Available ] ──► [ Assigned ] ──► [ In Use ] ──► [ Under Maintenance ]
                                      │
                         ┌────────────┼────────────┐
                         ▼            ▼            ▼
                   [ Under Repair ] [ Missing ] [ Retired/Disposed ]
```

#### Common State Change Actions:
- **Assign to Staff/Room**: Select asset → Click **Assign** → Select assigned teacher or room location. Status changes to `Assigned` or `In Use`.
- **Send for Repair**: If a laptop screen breaks:
  1. Open the asset record and click **Report Issue / Repair**.
  2. Describe the fault (e.g., *"Cracked LCD screen, power supply failure"*).
  3. Status automatically changes to `Under Repair`.
  4. Enter estimated repair cost and vendor details.
  5. Upon repair completion, click **Return to Service** to restore status to `In Use` or `Available`.
- **Scheduled Maintenance**: For annual PC servicing or 3D printer calibration, select items → Click **Schedule Maintenance** → Status changes to `Under Maintenance`.
- **Marking Missing Items**: If an item is missing during term-end stocktaking, mark status as `Missing`. This triggers an inventory review alert for administrators.
- **Retirement & Disposal**: Old computers or damaged items beyond economic repair are set to `Retired` or `Disposed` with formal write-off approval notes recorded.

### Generating Inventory Reports
1. Go to **Asset Reports**.
2. Select report type:
   - **Equipment by Location** (e.g., all active devices in *STEM Lab*).
   - **Maintenance & Repair Cost Summary**.
   - **Warranty Expiration Alert List**.
3. Click **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** for school board or department reporting.
