# CMS Content Management & User Guide

Welcome to the **SLGS Content Management System (CMS)** user guide. This document explains how school staff, editors, and student club members (such as the Multimedia Club and News Journal Club) create, edit, approve, publish, and manage content for the public Sierra Leone Grammar School website.

---

## 1. User Roles & Capabilities

The CMS uses role-based access to ensure student contributions are moderated while empowering editorial staff:

| Role | Who Has This Role? | What Can You Do? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Club Author / Contributor** | Members of Multimedia Club & News Journal Club | • Create new article drafts & announcements<br/>• Upload photo assets to the media library<br/>• Submit drafts for editor review |
| **Content Editor** | Designated staff & senior publication leads | • Review & moderate submitted student drafts<br/>• Edit article text, formatting, & image captions<br/>• Approve or return drafts with feedback notes<br/>• Manage static school pages |
| **CMS Administrator** | School Communications & IT Leads | • Final publishing approval to live public website<br/>• Unpublish or archive outdated articles<br/>• Rollback updates using revision history<br/>• Manage CMS user accounts & club memberships |

---

## 2. Getting Started & Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the CMS web portal (e.g. `http://cms.slgs.edu.sl` or `http://localhost:3002` during testing).
2. Click **Sign In** and enter your school email address and password.
3. Upon logging in, you will arrive at the **Editorial Dashboard**:
   - **My Drafts**: Articles and announcements you are currently editing.
   - **Review Queue** *(Editors & Admins only)*: Items awaiting editorial approval.
   - **Recently Published**: Live content visible on the public website.
   - **Quick Actions**: "Create New Article", "Upload Media", "Create Gallery".

---

## 3. How to Create, Edit, & Submit Articles

### Step 1: Create a New Draft
1. Click **+ New Article** from the sidebar or dashboard.
2. Fill in the article metadata:
   - **Title**: Clear, engaging title (e.g., *"SLGS Annual Sports Day 2026 Results"*).
   - **Category**: Select News, Sports, Academics, Club Update, or Announcement.
   - **Summary / Excerpt**: A brief 2-sentence preview displayed on the homepage.
   - **Featured Image**: Click **Select Image** to pick a photo from the Media Library or upload a new one.
3. Write your content using the Rich Text Editor:
   - Use headings (`Heading 2`, `Heading 3`) to organize sections.
   - Add bold text, bulleted lists, and blockquotes for highlights.

### Step 2: Save & Preview
- Click **Save Draft** at any time. Drafts are automatically saved as you write.
- Click **Preview** to view how your article will look on the public website before submitting.

### Step 3: Submit for Review (Authors & Club Members)
- Once your draft is complete, click **Submit for Review**.
- The article status changes from `Draft` → `Submitted`.
- The article enters the **Review Queue** for Content Editors. You can monitor editor comments or feedback from your dashboard.

---

## 4. How Editors Review & Moderate Content

*This section applies to Content Editors and CMS Administrators.*

1. Navigate to the **Review Queue** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click on any article marked `Submitted`.
3. Review the submission for:
   - Accuracy, tone, and alignment with school policies.
   - Spelling, grammar, and proper image formatting/captions.
4. Take an Action:
   - **Approve**: Mark the article as `Approved`. It is now ready for publishing.
   - **Request Edits (Reject)**: Add feedback notes (e.g., *"Please add names of the winning relay team members"*) and click **Return to Author**. The article status changes to `Rejected`, notifying the author to make changes and resubmit.
   - **Direct Edit**: Editors can directly edit typos or formatting before approving.

---

## 5. How to Publish, Edit Live Content, & Unpublish

*This section applies to CMS Administrators.*

### Publishing Approved Content
1. Navigate to the **Approved** tab.
2. Select the article and click **Publish Now** (or schedule a future publication time).
3. The status changes to `Published`. Within seconds, the content becomes live on the public school website (`slgs.edu.sl`).

### Editing Already Published Content
1. Go to **Published Articles** and click **Edit**.
2. Make necessary updates (e.g., updating a venue or date).
3. Click **Save & Update Live Content**.
4. **Revision History**: A new version snapshot is saved automatically. If an accidental mistake was made, click **Revision History** → **Restore This Version** to instantly roll back to an earlier revision.

### Unpublishing & Archiving Content
1. Go to **Published Articles**.
2. Select the article you wish to remove from public view.
3. Click **Unpublish / Archive**.
4. The article is removed from the public website immediately and returned to `Draft` or `Archived` state in the CMS database. It can be republished at any time.

## 6. Managing Photos, Media, & Galleries

### Uploading Photos & Media
1. Navigate to **Media Library** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Upload Media**. Select files from your computer (JPEG, PNG, or PDF).
3. **Important Information to Add**:
   - **Alt Text (Accessibility)**: Describe the image (e.g., *"SLGS Science Club presenting robotics project"*).
   - **Caption**: Brief descriptive caption displayed under the photo.
   - **Credit**: Photographer or club attribution (e.g., *"Photo by Multimedia Club"*).

### Creating Photo Galleries
1. Navigate to **Galleries** → **Create New Gallery**.
2. Enter the Gallery Title (e.g., *"Michaelmas Term Graduation Ceremony"*).
3. Click **Add Photos from Media Library** and select images.
4. Drag and drop photos to re-order them.
5. Click **Submit for Review** or **Publish Gallery**.
