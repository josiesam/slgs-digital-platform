# CMS Content Management & User Guide

Welcome to the **SLGS Content Management System (CMS)** user guide. This document explains how school staff, editors, and student club members (such as the Multimedia Club and News Journal Club) create, edit, approve, publish, and manage content for the public Sierra Leone Grammar School website.

---

## 1. User Roles & Capabilities

The CMS uses role-based access to ensure student contributions are moderated while empowering editorial staff:

| Role | Who Has This Role? | What Can You Do? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Club Author / Contributor** | Members of Multimedia Club & News Journal Club | • Create new article drafts & announcements<br/>• Upload photo assets to the media library<br/>• Submit drafts for editor review |
| **Content Editor / Reviewer** | Designated staff & senior publication leads | • Review & moderate submitted student drafts<br/>• Start & complete peer reviews with feedback notes<br/>• Approve or return drafts to authors<br/>• Manage static school pages |
| **CMS Administrator / Publisher** | School Communications & IT Leads | • Final publishing approval to live public website<br/>• Unpublish or archive outdated articles<br/>• Roll back updates using revision history<br/>• Manage CMS user accounts & club memberships |

---

## 2. Getting Started & Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the CMS web portal (e.g. `http://cms.slgs.edu.sl` or `http://localhost:3002` during testing).
2. Click **Sign In** and enter your school email address and password.
3. Upon logging in, you will arrive at the **Editorial Workspace**:
   - **Content Repository**: View all draft content items and revision histories.
   - **Editorial Kanban Desk**: Four column board (*Drafts & Revisions*, *Peer Review Queue*, *Final Approval Queue*, *Published / Live*).
   - **Access & System**: Manage clubs, custom roles, and audit logs.

---

## 3. How to Create, Edit, & Submit Articles

### Step 1: Create a New Draft
1. Click **+ New Draft** or navigate to the Content Repository.
2. Fill in the article details:
   - **Title**: Clear, engaging title (e.g., *"SLGS Annual Sports Day 2026 Results"*).
   - **URL Slug**: Lowercase URL slug (e.g., `annual-sports-day-2026`).
   - **Canonical Path**: Optional custom canonical path. If left blank, the system automatically assigns `/news/annual-sports-day-2026`.
   - **Summary**: A brief preview excerpt displayed on listings and cards.
   - **Body Content**: Detailed article content paragraphs.
   - **Owning Club**: Select your assigned club (e.g., News Journal Club).

### Step 2: Save Revisions
- Click **Save Draft**. Drafts create an immutable version snapshot in the revision history.

### Step 3: Submit for Review
- Click **Submit** on the item or via the Editorial Kanban Desk.
- The state changes from `Draft` → `Submitted`. The item appears in the **Peer Review Queue**.

---

## 4. How Editors Review & Moderate Content

*This section applies to Content Editors, Reviewers, and CMS Administrators.*

> ⚠️ **Author Independence Rule**: You cannot start review, complete review, or approve content that you authored yourself. Self-action attempts are automatically blocked server-side.

1. Open the **Editorial Kanban Desk** and look at the **Peer Review Queue**.
2. Click **Start Review** on an item marked `Submitted`. The status changes to `In Review`.
3. Evaluate the content for accuracy, tone, image alt text, and formatting.
4. Take an Action:
   - **Complete Review**: Enter your review comments in the comment box and click **Complete Review**. This records your review timestamp (`reviewedAt`) while keeping the item in review.
   - **Reject**: Enter return feedback notes (e.g., *"Please add names of winning relay team"*) and click **Reject**. The status changes to `Rejected`, notifying the author to make edits and resubmit.
   - **Approve**: Once a review has been completed by an independent reviewer, click **Approve**. The status changes to `Approved`.

---

## 5. How to Publish & Unpublish Content

*This section applies to CMS Publishers and Administrators.*

### Publishing Approved Content
1. Open the **Editorial Kanban Desk** and view the **Final Approval Queue** (`Approved` state).
2. Click **Publish**.
3. The status changes to `Published`, and `publishedAt` timestamp is recorded.
4. The item immediately projects into the public database read views and appears live on the public school website (`slgs.edu.sl`) under its canonical path (e.g. `/news/annual-sports-day-2026`).

### Unpublishing Content
1. View the **Published / Live** column on the Editorial Kanban Desk.
2. Click **Unpublish**.
3. The status returns to `Approved`. The item is immediately removed from the public website while retaining its revision history in CMS.

## 6. Managing Photos, Media, & Galleries

### Uploading Photos & Media
1. Navigate to **Media Library** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Initiate Upload**. Select image files (PNG, JPEG, WebP up to 10 MB).
3. Provide descriptive **Alt Text** for accessibility.
4. Uploads are direct-to-R2 via presigned URLs, verified server-side, and marked `available`.

### Creating Photo Galleries
1. Create a new draft item with type **Gallery**.
2. Open **Edit Draft & Media Details** → **Gallery Media Composition**.
3. Select available media assets from your library and arrange their sort order.
4. Submit, review, approve, and publish the gallery.
