# S.I.M.S. & ICT/STEM Asset Management (`apps/sims`)

> 📖 **Looking for the user guide?** Read the step-by-step **[S.I.M.S. & Asset Management User Guide](/docs/sims-user-guide)** for school administrators, form teachers, and ICT staff.

`apps/sims` is the private operational application designed for Sierra Leone Grammar School administrative leadership, academic staff, and ICT/STEM asset managers.

---

## Target Audience & Operational Boundaries

- **School Administrators**: Global identity lifecycle management, role delegation, academic session configuration, system audit logs.
- **Academic Staff**: Class rosters, daily student attendance logging, assessment recording.
- **ICT/STEM Staff**: Asset inventory tracking, lab equipment allocations, planned maintenance, repair workflows, procurement, and disposals.

---

## Identity & Access Policy

1. **Isolation from CMS & Web**: S.I.M.S. permissions are explicit and server-side checked. Having a CMS editor account grants **zero** S.I.M.S. privileges.
2. **Administrative Identity Ownership**: While `@slgs/auth` manages password hashing and session tokens, administrative user lifecycles and scope assignments are owned strictly by `apps/sims` via transaction-wrapped services in `@slgs/sims-domain`.
3. **Staff Identity Linking**: Staff records in `sims.staff` are domain entities that may optionally link to a Better Auth user account. No credentials or authentication logic exist in domain tables.

---

## Core School Operations

### 1. Student Records (`sims.students`)
- Master student directory containing registration numbers, full names, date of birth, gender, enrollment status, class assignments, and guardian contact details.
- Student records are administrative data entities rather than authenticating users.

### 2. Staff Management (`sims.staff`)
- Staff employment directory, academic designations, department assignments, and system authorization links.

### 3. Classes & Subjects (`sims.classes`, `sims.subjects`)
- Form levels, arms/sections, subject catalogs, and teacher-subject-class allocations.

### 4. Academic Sessions (`sims.academic_sessions`)
- School year and term definitions (e.g., *2025/2026 Academic Year — Michaelmas Term*).

### 5. Attendance Tracking (`sims.attendance`)
- **Occurrence-Time Context**: Attendance entries record class and session context at the exact moment of marking rather than relying on mutable current class pointers.
- **Immutable Correction Audit**: Any attendance modification preserves superseded historical records for compliance and auditability.

## ICT/STEM Asset & Inventory Management

A major capability within `apps/sims` is end-to-end asset management for the school's computing labs, robotics kits, networking infrastructure, and STEM equipment.

### Master Asset Metadata

Every tracked asset maintains rich operational metadata:

- **Identification**: Asset Tag / QR ID, Serial Number, Category, Manufacturer, Model.
- **Acquisition**: Purchase Date, Cost, Supplier, Warranty Expiration Date.
- **Location & Ownership**: Room / Facility Location, Assigned Staff/Department, Current Condition.
- **Documentation**: Uploaded user manuals, maintenance records, photos.

### 10-State Asset Lifecycle

Assets transition through 10 explicit lifecycle states enforced by `@slgs/sims-domain`:

```
           ┌──────────┐      Assign       ┌──────────┐
           │ Available │ ───────────────► │ Assigned │
           └────┬─────┘                   └────┬─────┘
                │                              │ Use
                ▼                              ▼
          ┌───────────┐                   ┌──────────┐
          │  In Use   │ ◄─────────────────┤ Retired  │
          └────┬──────┘                   └──────────┘
               │
    ┌──────────┼──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
    ▼          ▼                      ▼                      ▼
┌───────┐ ┌─────────┐          ┌──────────────┐       ┌────────────┐
│Repair │ │Missing  │          │ Maintenance  │       │ Disposed   │
└───────┘ └─────────┘          └──────────────┘       └────────────┘
```

| Lifecycle State | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Available** | Asset is in storage and ready for deployment or allocation. |
| **Assigned** | Allocated to a specific teacher, department, or lab room. |
| **In Use** | Actively deployed and utilized in academic/administrative workflows. |
| **Under Maintenance** | Undergoing routine scheduled maintenance or calibration. |
| **Under Repair** | Out of service undergoing active hardware repair. |
| **Missing** | Unaccounted for during physical inventory audit. |
| **Damaged** | Broken or non-functional awaiting inspection. |
| **Retired** | Phased out from primary use; retained for spare parts or secondary tasks. |
| **Disposed** | Formally written off, recycled, or sold according to school policy. |

### Supported Asset Categories

- **Computers & Laptops**: Desktop workstations, teacher laptops, student lab PCs.
- **Tablets & Mobile**: Student tablets, mobile inventory scanners.
- **Networking Infrastructure**: Switches, routers, Wi-Fi access points, UPS units.
- **STEM & Robotics**: Arduino kits, Raspberry Pi units, robotics sets, sensors, 3D printers.
- **Laboratory Equipment**: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology lab apparatus.
- **Peripherals & Consumables**: Projectors, printers, toner cartridges, cables.

## Operational Workflows

1. **Maintenance Logs**: Scheduled servicing dates, vendor contacts, and preventative maintenance checklists.
2. **Repair Workflow**: Damage intake → repair assignment → cost tracking → resolution & testing → return to service.
3. **Procurement Records**: Supplier contracts, purchase orders, delivery confirmation, and warranty registration.
4. **Disposal Auditing**: Formal write-off approval workflow with disposal reason logging.
